We congratulate Mrs [Susmita Ghosh](https://in.linkedin.com/in/susmita-ghosh-5223b8a1) on the successful defence of her doctoral research titled [**Enhancing Medical Image Analysis through Deep Learning: A Comprehensive Study on Classification, Segmentation, and Multitask Learning**](https://digitalcommons.isical.ac.in/doctoral-theses/626/). She has delivered an impressive defense, answering questions with clarity and confidence before his supervisor Prof. Swagatam Das, external examiner Prof. Pabitra Mitra (IIT, Kharagpur), and attendees.
